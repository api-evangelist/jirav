The financial model you build for a seed-stage client is not a smaller version of the model you build at Series B. It answers a different question, it is read by different people, and it fails in different ways. Advisory firms that treat the startup model as a single artifact that just accumulates rows over time end up rebuilding it from scratch at every round, usually under deadline pressure, and usually in the middle of diligence.
